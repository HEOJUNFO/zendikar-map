## Generation prompt

Create a polished high-resolution landscape creature design sheet for a dark fantasy first-person game inspired by Magic: the Gathering Zendikar. This is production 3D character concept reference, physically believable high-detail anatomy and PBR materials, not a cartoon or low-poly aesthetic. Five separate full-body creatures in five evenly spaced columns, enough breathing room, each 3/4 front view on the same subdued charcoal studio backdrop. Subtle warm/cool neutral studio illumination that reveals natural shadows; glowing magic stays small and does not flood the body with light. Design the five actual gameplay roles: (1) a lean terrifying ancient ruin skeleton warrior with anatomically accurate weathered skull, exposed bones, fragmentary aged bronze and carved stone armor, Kor-style triangular/diamond carvings and an old sword; (2) a Roil elemental instead of a cute green blob, asymmetrical translucent smoky amber mana mass wrapped around jagged obsidian stones and one eroded diamond hedron core, predatory and ancient, no smiling face; (3) a large realistic cavern spider with eight jointed hairy legs, layered chitin, small clustered eyes and subtle mossy blue-green mineral deposits, credible wall-climbing anatomy; (4) a predatory realistic vampire bat with folded and partly open membranous wings, dense dark fur, sharp believable muzzle and small faint turquoise mana fissures; (5) a imposing ancient drake boss with a long predatory head, strong anatomical neck and talons, large leathery wings, dark slate/obsidian scales and worn ridged horn crown, sparse carved hedron-like rune inlays, natural muscular proportions. Cohesive Zendikar Skyclave / Roil / ancient ruins visual language. Extremely detailed skin, stone, bone, cloth remnants, scales and wings. Grounded adult dark fantasy real-time character rendering quality. No giant heads, no baby dragons, no toy proportions, no cartoon slime, no box-built robot bodies, no branding, no text. All five silhouettes fully visible, uncropped wings and legs. Wide 2:1 composition, high resolution.

## Runtime constraints

Use the selected meshes and original licensed rigs as the actual game assets. Derived sculpting and texture baking must retain mesh provenance. Animation frames share their vertex order, UVs, material layers and index topology so the GPU interpolates poses without rebuilding mesh data each frame. Character albedo and roughness must receive the room's actual baked light probes. Only small spell effects may emit light; persistent body fills must not hide the room's darkness.
