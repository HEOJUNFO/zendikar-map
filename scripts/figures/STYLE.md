# Zendikar map figures — style guide (phase 1: ZEN mythic rare subjects)

The map (open the app, e.g. `pnpm dev` and http://localhost:5173/?phase=1) is a parchment fantasy map:
vellum land (#eae4d1), pale sea (#d9ded6) with ripple lines, ink-drawn mountains (vellum fill + thin dark ridge line + light hatch), tree crowns (small circles, translucent fill, green-grey ink), floating hedrons (stone fill, ink outline, facet lines), IM Fell English labels.

Figures are illustrations drawn ON the map the way old maps (Olaus Magnus' Carta Marina, Ortelius, Tolkien-style fan maps) draw sea monsters, beasts and people: a woodcut/engraving look, NOT cartoon, NOT flat icon, NOT realistic shading.

## Hard rules
- Monochrome ink line art on vellum: outlines with class `ink` (1.15px screen) and a few `ink-bold` accents (1.5px) for the silhouette's most important edges; interior form with `hatch` lines (0.6px), parallel or following form, used sparingly (engraving feel).
- Fills: `fill` (vellum) under every closed shape so the figure covers terrain behind it; `shade` (vellum-shade) for the shadow side; optional muted washes ONLY where the subject is defined by them: `stone` (hedrons, monuments, obsidian), `forest` (plant matter), `sea` (water splash), `fire` (flame — very muted ochre, never bright), `blood` (vampire accents, tiny), `gold` (a few accents), `dark` (small solid ink areas: eyes, mouth, deep shadow — tiny).
- Every stroke is screen-px constant (vector-effect), so geometry must read at small sizes: a strong, simple silhouette first; details second. Check the smallest scale the figure will be shown at (see "visible from").
- Coordinates: write the figure in its own coordinate box `viewBox: [minX, minY, width, height]` (use roughly 100 units for the longest side), `anchor: [x, y]` = the point that sits on the map location (feet/base for standing things, body centre for flying or swimming things). Only absolute path commands M L C Q A Z (and H V). Keep each figure under ~60 path parts and ~25k characters.
- Facing: standing figures face left or right in 3/4 view; no text inside figures; no frames; no drop shadows except an optional small ground shadow ellipse using `shade`.
- Caption: the app puts the name below the figure, else right, else left; if a regional detail drawn under a world figure has a tower, wall or other feature on that side, set `label: 'above' | 'right' | 'left' | 'below'` on its card in phase1.ts…phase5.ts (the side to try first at every zoom).
- The world map's scattered terrain glyphs (trees, marsh tufts, tundra, lava, ice, canyon scarps, period patterns) under a placed caption are dropped one by one at each zoom tier, and mountain-band glyphs (peaks, hills, snow peaks, mesas, crystals) have only the lines and fill inside the caption box knocked out, so the range stays (`src/map/glyphSpans.ts`). The box covers the caption at every zoom of its tier (font metrics in `src/map/captionMetrics.ts`), so a caption crossing glyphs is not a reason to move a figure. Regional-detail glyphs are dropped the same way at deep zoom (2026-10-31); only the detail's hand-drawn parts stay, so a detail subject's caption side is chosen to keep off walls, cliffs and hatching (`scripts/childmaps/STYLE.md`).
- Draw order between figures: the app draws figures in card order (phase1.ts…phase5.ts, rarity then collector number), so a later card's figure covers an earlier one where they overlap. A big figure that other figures must stand on or in front of (the Sea Gate hedron ring, `aligned-hedron-network`, under Ob Nixilis above its centre and Gideon on the rock in front of it) sets `under: true` on its card instead of moving the card: it is drawn before every other figure. The card keeps its place in the file and in the place panel; keyboard Tab follows the drawing order (SVG has no z-index), so it comes first there.
- Parts are drawn in order; put `fill`/`shade`/washes first, then `hatch`, then `ink`/`ink-bold` on top.
- The subject must read as THAT subject from its official description (card name/type/flavor and lore), not as a copy of the card art. Do not trace or copy the card illustration; draw an original map-style emblem of the subject.

## Preview
Open `scripts/figures/index.html?ids=<id>[,<id>…]&scales=0.53,1.6,2.6,5` in a browser (file:// works). Each figure is drawn on parchment at those map scales (px per map unit) next to the map's own symbols (mountains, trees, the 10px settlement marker, a 14px label). Judge it at every scale it will be shown at, then run `node scripts/figures/to_ts.mjs > src/map/figures.ts`.

## Timeline view (`?time=t`, `src/data/timeline.ts`)
A card is drawn at its set's time point (T1 ZEN·WWK, T2 ROE, T3 BFZ, T4 OGW) and, under "what exists persists" (CLAUDE.md '시점별 지도', 2026-10-20), at every later point unless the lore gives a reason it ended (died or destroyed, left Zendikar, consumed or collapsed with its spot) and every earlier point unless it gives a reason it began later (born, made, awakened, came to Zendikar — Eldrazi and their damage from T2). Scenes, spells and battle moments stay at their own point. That is written on the card in phase1.ts…phase5.ts, never in the art:
- `time.at` lists the points; `time.basis` (official) or `time.estimate` (this map's judgment, shown as '추정') explains every point the saga view does not draw and every point dropped inside the drawn span (V2, V11 — Korean, like `basis`/`estimate`; names in official Korean only, else English). `time.home` moves a card's own point (Sorin Markov → 2).
- `time.moved: { 3: { at, flip?, label?, childMap? } }` draws it elsewhere at that point only (same size): official sources place it elsewhere then, the period background removed its spot (dust, dead forest, dried bed, ruin, hidden place mark), it lost a spot contest, or it hid a place name. `childMap` names the regional detail it is drawn in at that point when the spot is outside its own (`null` = the world map). Spot contests never drop a point: precise spot > the card whose set point it is > carried on an official basis > carried by judgment (between two judgment carries, the nearest earlier set keeps the spot) — the loser moves. `time.label: { 3: 'left' }` changes only the caption side. `time.same` (one figure per point for the same being; same-name reprints follow the same rule — the set point wins, otherwise the nearest earlier card), `time.beside` (a symbol drawn only while its host is), `time.aloft` (in the sky over ground lost at that point).
- `node scripts/figures/to_ts.mjs` stops on V7 (at a point outside the saga span, the figure's base — its moved spot if moved — lies in white dust, chalk, the painted dead forest or a dried bed that is on at that point but not at its set point, unless it is `aloft`; the Oran-Rief place ellipse only swaps trees and does not count. Or the place it depicts is a ruin only at that point and it is neither moved nor aloft) and V8 (box overlaps at a time point: 0.3 of the smaller box, 0.05 when a moved figure is involved, any overlap of a world figure over a regional-detail subject). While carried points are still waiting for their spots, `TIMELINE_LAYOUT=warn` reports V8 on stderr and writes the files anyway; V7 always stops.
- It also writes each figure drawn outside its set's point once more into `src/map/figures-time/<band|detail id>-t<points>.ts` (table `groups.json`), so the timeline view does not download whole saga groups for long-lived subjects. Do not edit those files; `node scripts/qa/timeline-check.mjs` reports a stale table.
- After placing or moving figures, run `node scripts/qa/labels-check.mjs` (dev server): names may move but must not disappear. Never shrink a figure to make room.
