# Zendikar map figures — style guide (phase cards of every saga: ZEN·WWK·ROE, BFZ·OGW, ZNR, MOM·MAT)

The map (open the app, e.g. `pnpm dev` and http://localhost:5173/?saga=1&phase=1 — `?saga=s&phase=p` for each saga's phases, `?time=1..6` for the timeline view) is a parchment fantasy map:
vellum land (#eae4d1), pale sea (#d9ded6) with ripple lines, ink-drawn mountains (vellum fill + thin dark ridge line + light hatch), tree crowns (small circles, translucent fill, green-grey ink), floating hedrons (stone fill, ink outline, facet lines), IM Fell English labels.

Figures are illustrations drawn ON the map the way old maps (Olaus Magnus' Carta Marina, Ortelius, Tolkien-style fan maps) draw sea monsters, beasts and people: a woodcut/engraving look, NOT cartoon, NOT flat icon, NOT realistic shading.

## Hard rules
- Monochrome ink line art on vellum: outlines with class `ink` (1.15px screen) and a few `ink-bold` accents (1.5px) for the silhouette's most important edges; interior form with `hatch` lines (0.6px), parallel or following form, used sparingly (engraving feel).
- Fills: `fill` (vellum) under every closed shape so the figure covers terrain behind it; `shade` (vellum-shade) for the shadow side; optional muted washes ONLY where the subject is defined by them: `stone` (hedrons, monuments, obsidian), `forest` (plant matter), `sea` (water splash), `fire` (flame — very muted ochre, never bright), `blood` (vampire accents, tiny), `gold` (a few accents), `dark` (small solid ink areas: eyes, mouth, deep shadow — tiny).
- Every stroke is screen-px constant (vector-effect), so geometry must read at small sizes: a strong, simple silhouette first; details second. Check the smallest scale the figure will be shown at (see "visible from").
- Coordinates: write the figure in its own coordinate box `viewBox: [minX, minY, width, height]` (use roughly 100 units for the longest side), `anchor: [x, y]` = the point that sits on the map location (feet/base for standing things, body centre for flying or swimming things). Only absolute path commands M L C Q A Z (and H V). Keep each figure under ~60 path parts and ~25k characters.
- Facing: standing figures face left or right in 3/4 view; no text inside figures; no frames; no drop shadows except an optional small ground shadow ellipse using `shade`.
- Caption: the app puts the name below the figure, else right, else left; if a regional detail drawn under a world figure has a tower, wall or other feature on that side, set `label: 'above' | 'right' | 'left' | 'below'` on its card in phase1.ts…phase8.ts (the side to try first at every zoom).
- The world map's scattered terrain glyphs (trees, marsh tufts, tundra, lava, ice, canyon scarps, period patterns) under a placed caption — and, since 2026-11-02, under every placed place, card and region name (sea and lake names included — they are region labels too; sea marks such as ice floes, whirls and reefs are not scattered glyphs and never drop, so a water name only clears the few glyphs where its box reaches the shore) — are dropped one by one at each zoom tier, and mountain-band glyphs (peaks, hills, snow peaks, mesas, crystals) either keep their fill whole and lose only the ridge line inside the caption box or are dropped whole, so the range stays without square notches (on forest/lava/period washes only a peak the caption grazes stays — over 85% of the ridge left, top outside the box, no short orphan piece; crystals, whose fill is tinted, follow that rule on any ground spire by spire (each spire is its own closed outline): a spire the box cuts stays only with its tip outside the box, over 85% of its outline and no orphan piece, otherwise that spire goes with its fill and hatching, untouched spires stay as drawn, and the cluster stays while its remaining spires hold over half of its outline; on parchment, where the fill matches the ground, short orphan pieces go and a peak stays only while its top is left and each slope keeps over 60% of its length; a peak that loses all its hatching or snow line is dropped too, so no bare '^' or one-legged peak floats by a name — hatch strokes the box touches go whole, not clipped, to leave no crumbs; 2026-11-03) (`src/map/glyphSpans.ts`). The box covers the caption's ink (width and per-name ink height from `src/map/captionMetrics.ts`) at every zoom of a 2^(1/4) zoom step inside its tier, so it stays close to the text and a caption crossing glyphs is not a reason to move a figure. Regional-detail glyphs are dropped the same way at deep zoom (2026-10-31); only the detail's hand-drawn parts stay, so a detail subject's caption side is chosen to keep off walls, cliffs and hatching (`scripts/childmaps/STYLE.md`).
- Draw order between figures: the app draws figures in card order (phase1.ts…phase8.ts in file order — phase1·4·5 grouped by rarity, phase2·3·6·7·8 in collector-number order, re-carried cards (`…-2020`, `…-2023`) at the end), so a later card's figure covers an earlier one where they overlap. A big figure that other figures must stand on or in front of (the Sea Gate hedron ring, `aligned-hedron-network`, under Ob Nixilis above its centre and Gideon on the rock in front of it) sets `under: true` on its card instead of moving the card: it is drawn before every other figure. The card keeps its place in the file and in the place panel; keyboard Tab follows the drawing order (SVG has no z-index), so it comes first there.
- Parts are drawn in order; put `fill`/`shade`/washes first, then `hatch`, then `ink`/`ink-bold` on top.
- The subject must read as THAT subject from its official description (card name/type/flavor and lore), not as a copy of the card art. Do not trace or copy the card illustration; draw an original map-style emblem of the subject.
- Double-faced cards: draw the front face only. A modal land face (`land`, ZNR) is not a figure — it goes in the panel of the place it depicts, so the front's figure stands beside that place's mark (within 40 units plus its extent, `src/data/landFace.ts`). A transforming back face (`back`, MOM battles and transforming creatures) is never drawn; it appears only in the card panel. A battle is a scene (`isEffect`).
- A card that names a character but is not that character (a spell, a judgment, a bond) does not draw the character again: draw a symbol beside the character's figure (Iona's Judgment, Reckless Handling, Nahiri's Resolve).

## File format (one file per figure): art/<card id>.js — the id must equal the card id in src/data/phase1.ts … phase8.ts
Phase files use one running number across sagas: phase1–3 Saga 1 (ZEN, WWK, ROE), phase4–5 Saga 2 (BFZ, OGW), phase6 Saga 3 (ZNR), phase7–8 Saga 4 (MOM, MAT). A same-name reprint gets its own id with the set suffix (`lotus-cobra-znr`, `negate-znr`); a card of an earlier saga carried again into a later one (the printed card itself, not a reprint, with `sagaSet` on its card) gets the earlier id plus the year (`tazri-beacon-of-unity-2023`) and its own art file, which may add only what the new spot needs (the floating ledge under Akiri and Orah in 2023). A card first printed outside the Zendikar story sets (Commander, core sets, Modern Horizons, Jumpstart, Foundations, Planechase — `src/data/extraSets.ts`, user decision 2026-11-06) sits in the phase file of its scene's era with `sagaSet` set to that era's story set and keeps the plain Scryfall front-face id (`omnath-locus-of-the-roil`, `anowon-the-ruin-thief` — the latter took over the drawing of the removed `anowon-the-ruin-sage-2020`).

## Preview
Open `scripts/figures/index.html?ids=<id>[,<id>…]&scales=0.53,1.6,2.6,5` in a browser (file:// works). Each figure is drawn on parchment at those map scales (px per map unit) next to the map's own symbols (mountains, trees, the 10px settlement marker, a 14px label). Judge it at every scale it will be shown at, then run `node scripts/figures/to_ts.mjs > src/map/figures.ts`.

## Saga view (`?saga=s&phase=p`, `src/data/sagas.ts`)
- Within a saga, phases stack; a new saga starts empty (only cards carried again on an official basis for its year come back, at their new spot). `until` (the last phase inside its saga) ends a figure whose subject is officially gone from that spot or state by the next phase, together with any symbol drawn beside it; the next phase's look comes only from that phase's cards, never from a second spot on the same card.
- `to_ts.mjs` writes each figure into a group named by its running phase number: `world`, `world-mid`, `world-small`, `world-near-x<col>y<row>` and `<detail id>` for phase 1, and the same names with `-2` … `-8` for later phases (`world-6`, `world-small-7`, `coralhelm-8`). Regional-detail clearings and hand-drawn parts are gated with `phase`, `phase2` … `phase8` (`scripts/childmaps/STYLE.md`).

## Timeline view (`?time=t`, `src/data/timeline.ts`)
A card is drawn at its set's time point (T1 2009 ZEN·WWK, T2 2010 ROE, T3 2015 BFZ, T4 2016 OGW, T5 2020 ZNR, T6 2023 MOM·MAT — a re-carried card and a card from outside the story sets count from its `sagaSet`, not its printed set) and, under "what exists persists" (CLAUDE.md '시점별 지도', 2026-10-20), at every later point unless the lore gives a reason it ended (died or destroyed, left Zendikar, consumed or collapsed with its spot) and every earlier point unless it gives a reason it began later (born, made, awakened, came to Zendikar — Eldrazi and their damage from T2). Scenes, spells and battle moments stay at their own point. That is written on the card in phase1.ts…phase8.ts, never in the art:
- `time.at` lists the points; `time.basis` (official) or `time.estimate` (this map's judgment, shown as '추정') explains every point the saga view does not draw and every point dropped inside the drawn span (V2, V11 — Korean, like `basis`/`estimate`; names in official Korean only, else English). `time.home` moves a card's own point (Sorin Markov → 2). From T5 (`PER_CARD_FROM`) every existing subject drawn at the point before gets a decision per card: carried (the point in `time.at`) or `time.gone` with the reason for that year. `time.at: []` draws a card at no point: only for a card whose set point's background already contradicts its scene or state, i.e. one the saga view ends with `until` before that background (the MOM invasion scenes, compleated forms and their invasion-time spots against the post-war T6 background); the reason names the year.
- `time.moved: { 3: { at, flip?, label?, childMap? } }` draws it elsewhere at that point only (same size): official sources place it elsewhere then, the period background removed its spot (dust, dead forest, dried bed, the 2023 dead-metal plain, ruin, hidden place mark), it lost a spot contest, or it hid a place name. `childMap` names the regional detail it is drawn in at that point when the spot is outside its own (`null` = the world map). Spot contests never drop a point: precise spot > the card whose set point it is > carried on an official basis > carried by judgment (between two judgment carries, the nearest earlier set keeps the spot) — the loser moves. `time.label: { 3: 'left' }` changes only the caption side. Spots are data per point: no point inherits an earlier point's move, so a T6 spot repeats the T5 one in `time.moved[6]` only while the T5 reason still holds in 2023, and `time.estimate` names that reason with the year (keeping the 2020 spot is not a reason). `time.same` (one figure per point for the same being — a group id, or a list of ids when one card shows two beings, as Drana and Linvala `['drana', 'linvala']`; same-name reprints follow the same rule — the set point wins, otherwise the nearest earlier card), `time.beside` (a symbol drawn only while its host is), `time.aloft` (in the sky over ground lost at that point).
- `node scripts/figures/to_ts.mjs` stops on V7 (at a point outside the saga span, or at any point where `time.moved` places it, the figure's base — its moved spot if moved — lies in white dust, chalk, the painted dead forest, the dead-metal plain or a dried bed that is on at that point but not at its set point (a moved spot is checked even if that ground is on at its set point, unless the card's own saga spot is in the same ground), unless it is `aloft`; the Oran-Rief place ellipse only swaps trees and does not count. Or the figure's base at that point (its moved spot if moved) stands at a place it depicts that is a ruin only at that point (`standsAtRuin`; a moved spot passes only when the ruin predates the card and the place was never whole, as with the pre-2020 skyclaves (`ruinBeforeCard`), or when it is nearer another place's mark), or sits within 3 (detail) / 8 (world) units of any mark that is a ruin at that point; `aloft` skips both) and V8 (box overlaps at a time point: 0.3 of the smaller box, 0.05 when a moved figure is involved, any overlap of a world figure over a regional-detail subject). While carried points are still waiting for their spots, `TIMELINE_LAYOUT=warn` reports V8 on stderr and writes the files anyway; V7 always stops.
- It also writes each figure drawn outside its set's point once more into `src/map/figures-time/<band|detail id>-t<points>.ts` (table `groups.json`), so the timeline view does not download whole saga groups for long-lived subjects. Do not edit those files; `node scripts/qa/timeline-check.mjs` reports a stale table.
- After placing or moving figures, run `node scripts/qa/labels-check.mjs --time 1,2,3,4,5,6 --lang en,ko` (dev server): names may move but must not disappear. Never shrink a figure to make room.
